# DnD 5.5e All-in-One BEYOND

[DnD 5.5e All-in-One BEYOND](https://github.com/Yoonmoonsik/bg3dnd) is a Baldur's Gate 3 mod that packs a broad set of D&D 5.5e-inspired rules, player options, spells, feats, equipment, and class updates into a single all-in-one mod.

For deeper gameplay notes, class details, spell behavior, item locations, and community-maintained documentation, see the [Wiki](https://github.com/Yoonmoonsik/bg3dnd/wiki).

To search every spell and feat in the mod by filters, use the [data browser](https://yoonmoonsik.github.io/bg3dnd/). It is generated from the mod's game data.

> Note: The Wiki is maintained by community contributors and is not officially written or managed by the mod developer. Some information may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate.

## Rules and Actions

- **Common Actions**: Distract, Dodge, Equipping Weapons, Extra Attack (including War Magic integration), Hide, Jump, Shove, Topple
- **Conditions**: Bloodied, Down, Prone
- **Rest and Recovery**: Hit Point Dice, Inspiration from Background
- **Spellcasting**: One Spell Slot Spell per Turn, Spell Scroll Usage Limitation
- **Equipment and Items**: Heavy Armor Strength Requirement, Heavy Weapon Ability Requirement, Weapon Mastery

## Species

See [Species](https://github.com/Yoonmoonsik/bg3dnd/wiki/Species).

- Aasimar, Changeling, Goliath, and Kalashtar are available as Human subspecies options. Eladrin, Ice Elf, and Shadar-kai are available as Elven Lineage options. Dhampir can be taken as a Human, Elf, Drow, Tiefling, Dwarf, Halfling, Gnome, or Orc subspecies.
- Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Githyanki, Gnome, Half Elf, Halfling, Human, Orc, and Tiefling are supported.
- Several species include ancestry or lineage options, including Dragonborn Draconic Ancestry, Elf and Half Elf sub-lineages, Goliath Giant Ancestry, and Tiefling legacy options.

## Support the Mod

This mod is developed and maintained for free, and it always will be. If you would like to support continued development, you can do so here:

- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/yoonmoonsik)
- [Toonation](https://toon.at/donate/yoonmoonsick) (for supporters in Korea)

Contributions go toward purchasing the official D&D rulebooks that this mod is based on, so that new content can be implemented accurately from the source material.

Support is entirely voluntary and grants no special access, priority requests, or exclusive content. Sharing feedback, reporting bugs, and helping others in Discussions are just as valuable.
